Mr Sanjay Ghodawat
Founder & Chairman of Sanjay Ghodawat Group (SGG)
Meet Mr Sanjay Ghodawat, a visionary Indian entrepreneur and philanthropist, leading the charge at Sanjay Ghodawat Group (SGG), one of India’s fastest-growing conglomerates. With a degree in mechanical engineering, he’s not just a business mind but also a licensed pilot for helicopters, planes, and gliders. Mr Sanjay Ghodawat’s unstoppable determination and diverse expertise drive SGG’s impressive growth, solidifying his legacy as a trailblazing entrepreneur in the Indian business scene. In 1992, Mr Sanjay Ghodawat laid the foundation for SGG, embarking on a dynamic journey that swiftly transformed the group. Under his guidance, SGG ventured into a spectrum of high-value industries, including Aviation, Consumer Products, Education, Energy, Mining, Realty, Retail, and Textiles. Today, SGG is like a big family with over 10,000 dedicated team members. Our goal is simple but ambitious: to become the best, the biggest, and the most valuable conglomerate
