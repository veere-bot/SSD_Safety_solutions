Regulatory & Technical FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about NBC regulations, FM-200 suppression, Fire NOCs, and Pro Delta AMCs.

How often does a commercial building need Fire Safety NOC renewal in Bengaluru?

In Karnataka, Fire Safety Advice and NOC compliance require annual or biennial verification depending on building classification (high-rise commercial vs industrial). Under NBC 2016 regulations, functional audit certificates must be submitted annually. Pro Delta provides complete AMC and audit assistance to obtain Karnataka Fire & Emergency Services NOC renewals.

What is the difference between FM-200 and CO2 Gas Suppression for server rooms?

FM-200 (HFC-227ea) is a clean agent safe for occupied spaces; it extinguishes fire rapidly without reducing oxygen to dangerous levels for humans. High Pressure CO2 suppresses fire by displacing oxygen and is extremely effective for unoccupied electrical panels/generator rooms, but CO2 must not be discharged in occupied rooms due to asphyxiation risk.

What is included in Pro Delta's Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC)?

Our AMC covers quarterly visits to test main electric & diesel hydrant pumps, main panel battery condition, pressure switch setpoints, sprinkler flow switches, alarm detector testing, hose box reel inspection, hydro-testing of extinguishers, and 24/7 breakdown callouts within 2 hours in Bengaluru metro.

Does Pro Delta export fire safety equipment overseas?

Yes. Operating under the brand name "DELTA®", Pro Delta Fire Safety Systems exports ISO-certified fire hydrant valves, RRL hoses, sprinkler heads, addressable control panels, and portable extinguishers to GCC countries (UAE, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Oman), Europe, and South Asia.

What fire safety equipment is compulsory for industrial manufacturing facilities?

According to National Building Code (NBC Part 4) and Factory Act norms, industrial facilities over 500 sq. meters require a combined internal & external yard hydrant network, diesel standby pump automation, automatic sprinklers in storage bays, portable ABC & CO2 extinguishers, and an addressable fire detection system.
