Mr. Gangolu Dasu
Founder of SSD Fire & Safety Solutions

Meet Mr. Gangolu Dasu, an entrepreneur whose journey from a humble background reflects determination, hard work, and a commitment to building a business with purpose. With a B.Tech in Electrical and Electronics Engineering, he brings a strong technical foundation to the fire and safety industry.

In 2017, Mr. Dasu established SSD Fire & Safety Solutions, beginning a journey focused on providing fire protection and safety solutions. Guided by his engineering background and entrepreneurial spirit, he has worked to grow the company and support safer environments for its clients.

His story is a reminder that ambition, persistence, and a willingness to learn can help turn a modest beginning into a meaningful enterprise.
